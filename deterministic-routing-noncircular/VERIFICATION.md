# Deterministic routing, non-circular: verification (deterministic-routing-noncircular)

**Verdict: VERIFIED-WITH-FIXES.** Every headline number in `RESULTS.md` that can be reached without an engine or a model call reproduces. An independent script that imports nothing from the bench recomputes every median and CV from the raw run lists, re-checks every answer against `results/oracle.json` and the anchor ground truth with its own comparator, rebuilds the per-cell truth, and re-scores S1, S2, S3, the three baselines, H4, H5, H6 and the LLM table. It agrees with the committed `results/scores.json` on every summary count and on all 504 corpus rows of S1, S2 and S3 (pick, outcome, best engine, acceptable set, cliff engines, regret, shape), and `score.py` and `posthoc_empty_sum.py`, re-run in a scratch copy, regenerate `scores.json` and `scores_posthoc_empty_sum.json` byte-identical. No pre-registered verdict changes. One headline count depends on a disclosed deviation: the "3 catastrophic picks" are 3 as scored and 2 under the frozen rule, because the pre-registration said to exclude a query the oracle fails on, and `join_3to4_09` is that query; H2 is falsified either way. H3 is called "falsified on the cliff clause" when the frozen regret pricing fails its regret clause too.
